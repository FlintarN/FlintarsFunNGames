# Flintar's Fun 'n' Games

A whole games room in one window, styled like WoW's own. A **Casino** for your group, where every number comes from the game's own `/roll` so nobody can fake a result, and an **Arcade** of just-for-fun games: Hearthstone against the computer or a friend, Warcraft III, Tetris, Battleship, Agar.io and more, with guild and realm leaderboards.

Type `/fng` to open it, or click the dice on your minimap.

## Casino

Play for gold, silver or copper with your party or raid.

- **Death Roll**: take turns rolling lower and lower. Roll a 1 and you pay.
- **High-Low**: the classic. Everyone rolls once; the lowest pays the highest the difference.
- **Poker**: Texas Hold'em for 2-6 players, with everyone's portrait at the table and cards that fly across it and turn over.
- **Blackjack**: beat the dealer to 21. Hit, stand or double; blackjack pays 3 to 2.
- **Slot Machine**: one player is the house, everyone else pulls the lever. Optional caps on losses and the house bankroll.
- **Roulette**: put your chips down, throw the ball, watch it drop. A number pays 35 to 1.
- **Raffle**: buy tickets, one is drawn with a real `/roll`, the winner takes the pot.

**Nobody can cheat.** Every roll is a real `/roll` the whole group sees; the addon only clicks it for you. Your addon checks every roll the host reports against what your own game saw. Poker and Blackjack decks are sealed before the deal, cut with a real `/roll` and checked by everyone afterwards.

**Pay later.** The **Settle up** tab keeps a running tab with each player ("Bob owes you 1g 20s"). When someone pays you in a trade, the addon notices and crosses it off. It only reads the trade and never touches it.

## Arcade

No betting, just games. Open a lobby to your group, your guild, the whole realm, or friends with a private code.

### Hearthstone

The card game, in WoW, against the computer or **a friend (PvP)**.

- Play **all nine classes**: Jaina, Thrall, Garrosh, Malfurion, Rexxar, Uther, Anduin, Valeera and Gul'dan, each with the original basic cards (real costs, stats and text) and their hero power.
- Minions show their **WoW creature** as a live 3D model: Murloc Raider, Boulderfist Ogre, Core Hound, War Golem and more.
- Taunt, Charge, Divine Shield, Windfury, Freeze, Spell Damage, Battlecries, Overload, hero powers and fatigue.
- **Build your own decks**: class and neutral cards, two copies each, 30 cards.
- **Play**: queue up and get matched with someone on your realm, like the real thing.
- **PvP with friends**: open a lobby for your group, guild, realm or a private code, pick your heroes and decks and play. Nobody sees the other hand, there's a mulligan, a turn timer, concede, rematch and a PvP leaderboard.
- A Hearthstone-style board: tavern table, hero portraits in gold frames, the oval End Turn button. Drag cards onto the board, watch spells fly and minions lunge.

### Warcraft III

A small real-time strategy game against the computer: Human or Orc, workers gathering gold and lumber, farms, barracks, lumber mills and towers, an army of Footmen and Riflemen (or Grunts and Troll Headhunters). Select with a box, right-click to command, attack-move with A, and watch the minimap: the computer attacks in growing waves.

### Play together

- **Tic-Tac-Toe**: X against O, rematches keep the score.
- **Battleship**: hide your fleet, take turns firing. Boards are sealed so nobody can move a ship.
- **Agar.io**: one big arena. Eat dots, grow, swallow your guildmates.

### Solo, with leaderboards

**Tetris** (by the official rules: hold, T-spins, back-to-back), **Snake**, **2048**, **Minesweeper**, **Flappy Bird**, **Wordle** (with words from Azeroth), **Space Shooter**, **Candy Crush** and **Angry Birds**. Your best scores go on leaderboards for you, your guild and your realm.

## Also

- **Fullscreen** for Warcraft III and Hearthstone.
- Every game has a **Rules** button explaining it in plain words; poker has **Hand rankings** too.
- **Practice with bots** in every multiplayer game: they join and play by themselves.
- Games keep running while you switch tabs or close the window. Right-click a game's tab to close it.
- Statistics: wins, losses and net gold per game.
- Settings: mute it, resize the window, hide the minimap button, clear your data.
- Never posts to public chat.

## Commands

- `/fng` - open or close the window (`/funngames` and `/gamble` work too)
- `/fng popup` - turn the automatic pop-up on or off
- `/fng mute` - turn the sounds off or on
- `/fng reset` - move the window back to the middle of the screen

Everyone who wants to play together needs the addon. Works on **Retail** and **WoW Forever**.
