# Changelog

## 1.1.0 (not released yet)

### New
- **Blizzard Games**: a third section on the home page, next to Casino and Arcade, for Hearthstone and Warcraft III.
- **Warcraft III** (Arcade): a small real-time strategy game against the computer. Units are their WoW creatures as live 3D models (walking, fighting, dying). Workers behave like Warcraft III: one in the gold mine at a time, harvesters walk through each other, a building only goes up while its worker stays (peons build from inside). Call to Arms (Militia) and Battle Stations (burrows), Back to Work, Hold Position, Return Resources, rally points with a flag, an Idle worker button and Warcraft III hotkeys with a Build menu. Three difficulty levels (Easy, Normal, Hard; Hard gets +25% gold and lumber per trip like Warcraft III's Insane AI). Two expansion gold mines. Humans can build a Lumber Mill (L), a lumber drop-off to put near the trees. Guard Tower and Watch Tower (T) shoot enemies in range; Altar of Kings and Altar of Storms (A) can be built (heroes come later). New models: warfront mage towers for the towers, the warfront orc barracks, and altars. Ground, trees, the gold mine and the buildings now come from the WoW game files: Elwynn grass, Elwynn trees, the Elwynn gold mine, a Westfall church (Town Hall), windmill (Farm) and blacksmith (Barracks), and Durotar orc tents for the Orc buildings. Play Human or Orc: gather gold and lumber with your workers, build farms and barracks, train Footmen and Riflemen (or Grunts and Troll Headhunters) and destroy every enemy building. Shift-queued orders (several buildings in a row, paid up front, refunded if cancelled), double-click to select a unit type, control groups (Ctrl+number), units and buildings drawn in depth order. Box selection, right-click commands, attack-move, rally points, a minimap and a Warcraft-style command card. The computer builds up and attacks in growing waves.
- **Warcraft III: sound.** The real Warcraft III voices still in WoW's files (Peasant, Peon, Footman, Grunt, Rifleman, Tauren: "Ready to work!", "Zug zug", "Job's done"...) and WoW NPC voices of the same race for every other unit and hero: they answer when selected, acknowledge orders, report when trained, and get annoyed if you keep clicking. Sword, axe, gun, arrow, siege and magic hits; death cries; buildings collapsing; a sound for every spell; level-up; "under attack" and "not enough gold" warnings.
- **Settings: two sound switches.** **Game sounds** (was Sounds): everything inside the games, Warcraft III's voices, battles and warnings included. **Alerts** (was Your-turn alert): your turn and disconnects, even with Game sounds off.
- **Warcraft III: looks.** Heroes, Knights, Tauren, Kodos, siege and riders are bigger than footmen; flyers fly higher with a shadow below; Avatar makes the hero bigger and stone grey, Bladestorm spins him round with his blade out, Bloodlust makes units bigger and faster, Slow slows them, Banish and Wind Walk make them see-through, stunned units freeze. The Catapult has a model now.
- **Warcraft III: Showcase** (main menu, or /wcdemo): every building, unit and hero on one map with their names, all yours to try: nothing moves, fights or casts unless you tell it to. Heroes know every ability; health, mana and cooldowns refill; Target Dummies to attack and cast on. Nothing is saved or counted.
- **Warcraft III: spell effects** from WoW's own spell models: Blizzard, Flame Strike and Earthquake on the ground for as long as they last; heals, lightning, Hex, Siphon Mana, Thunder Clap, War Stomp, summons and the rest where they land; stun swirls, Divine Shield, Avatar, Bladestorm, Banish, Inner Fire, Slow, Bloodlust and Reincarnation on the units while they last; Devotion, Brilliance and Endurance Aura under the hero; level-ups, potions and Town Portal.
- **Warcraft III: Cancel** a building going up (75% back, like Warcraft III), an upgrade, or the last unit in training, from the command card.
- **Warcraft III: every hero and unit has its own look** (a slow model frame could give one creature's look to the next, so the Paladin, Archmage and Priest all looked alike).
- **Warcraft III: a main menu** like Hearthstone's: Single Player (race and difficulty), Find an Opponent (race, then the realm queue) and Play a Friend, with Back to the game. The bar's Menu button opens it. Greyed-out buttons still show their tooltip (what they need). Units are smaller next to the buildings, and their models get more room, so heads and weapons aren't cut off.
- **Warcraft III: the full Human and Orc armies.** Knights, Priests, Sorceresses, Siege Engines, Flying Machines, Mortar Teams, Gryphon and Dragonhawk Riders; Catapults, Raiders, Kodo Beasts, Wind Riders, Troll Batriders, Shamans, Witch Doctors and Tauren; from the Arcane Sanctum, Workshop, Gryphon Aviary, Spirit Lodge, Beastiary and Tauren Totem. Flyers (only ranged attacks reach them), splash damage, siege that only hits buildings, casters that cast by themselves (Heal, Inner Fire, Slow, Bloodlust, Healing Ward), War Drums. Shops (Arcane Vault, Voodoo Lounge) sell potions, Town Portal, boots, claws and rings to the hero standing next to them; a 6-slot bag. Upkeep above 50 and 80 food. A 4 x 3 command card like Warcraft III.
- **Warcraft III: heroes.** Altars train the eight Human and Orc heroes (Paladin, Archmage, Mountain King, Blood Mage; Blademaster, Far Seer, Tauren Chieftain, Shadow Hunter), at most three (the 2nd needs a Keep, the 3rd a Castle). Levels 1 to 10 from kills nearby, strength/agility/intelligence, mana, a skill point a level (Hero Abilities, O), all four abilities each as in Warcraft III (ultimates at level 6): heals, stuns, auras, summons, area spells, Wind Walk, Mirror Image, Reincarnation and more. Fallen heroes are revived at the altar with their level and skills. The computer trains heroes, learns and casts too.
- **Warcraft III: tech and upgrades.** Town Hall > Keep > Castle and Great Hall > Stronghold > Fortress; requirements (Riflemen need a Blacksmith, Headhunters a War Mill, a Castle an Altar); research in buildings: Blacksmith (swords, gunpowder, plating), Lumber Mill (lumber harvesting, masonry), War Mill (new: Orc lumber drop-off with weapons, armour and Reinforced Defenses), Long Rifles, Berserker Strength, Troll Regeneration; Scout Tower upgrades to Guard Tower; Warcraft III damage and armour types. Locked buttons say what they need. The computer researches and upgrades too (not on Easy).
- **Warcraft III PvP**: play a friend (group, guild, realm or a private code) or Find an opponent on your realm. Both pick a race, then both addons run the same game in lockstep: only your orders travel (four times a second, by whisper), and a fingerprint of the game is compared every five seconds. Waiting for a slow player shows on screen; after 45 seconds of silence you can claim the victory. Surrender, rematch, your PvP record and a PvP leaderboard.
- **Warcraft III: fog of war.** Black where you've never been, dimmed where you've been but see nothing now. Enemy units show only in sight, enemy buildings stay once found; on the minimap too.
- **Hearthstone PvP**: "Play a friend" opens a lobby (group, guild, realm or a private code, or practice against a bot). Both pick a hero and deck; the host's addon runs the game and checks every move, nobody sees the other's hand (it comes by whisper), 75-second turns (miss two in a row and you lose), concede, rematch, your own PvP record and a PvP leaderboard.
- **Hearthstone: a main menu like the real one** (Play, Solo Adventures, Play a Friend, My Collection, Leaderboard) and a **realm queue**: Play picks your hero and deck, then finds someone else looking on your realm and starts the game by itself ("Finding Opponent...", then VS).
- **Hearthstone: mulligan.** Every game (PvP and against the computer) starts by showing your opening cards: click the ones to swap, then Confirm.
- **Hearthstone: leaderboards** on the start screen: wins against the computer and PvP wins, for your guild or realm.
- Hearthstone heroes: adult King Anduin, Valeera Sanguinar and the orc Gul'dan.
- **Fullscreen** for Warcraft III and Hearthstone: a button next to Rules fills the screen with the game (click again for the window). Remembered.
- **Hearthstone: all nine classes.** Malfurion (Druid), Rexxar (Hunter), Uther (Paladin), Anduin (Priest), Valeera (Rogue) and Gul'dan (Warlock) join Jaina, Thrall and Garrosh, who now has his Warrior cards. Each class has its original basic cards, hero power and a basic deck, and every minion shows its WoW creature.
- New rules for them: **weapons** (the hero attacks, durability, Truesilver's heal, Deadly Poison), targets like "a damaged minion" or "3 or less Attack", Sap and Vanish, Mind Control, Hunter's Mark, Humility, Divine Spirit, Corruption, discards, Mind Vision, Wild Growth, Multi-Shot and Cleave, Kill Command's Beast bonus, Warsong Commander, Starving Buzzard, Tundra Rhino, Timber Wolf and Northshire Cleric.

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-06)

The first public release. Works on **Retail** and **WoW Forever**.

### Casino (your party or raid, real `/roll`s)
- **Death Roll**, **High-Low**, **Poker** (Texas Hold'em, 2-6 players), **Blackjack**, **Slot Machine**, **Roulette** and **Raffle**.
- Fair by design: every number is a real `/roll` the whole group sees, checked by every player's addon; card decks are sealed before the deal and checked afterwards.
- Gold, silver and copper bets; **Settle up** keeps a running tab per player and notices payments in trades (read only).
- Disconnects: the host can skip a player who dropped, and bring them back.

### Arcade (no betting)
- **Hearthstone** against the computer: Jaina, Thrall or Garrosh with the original basic cards, WoW creature models on the cards, a Hearthstone-style board in a bigger window, drag and drop, spell animations, and a **deck builder**.
- **Tic-Tac-Toe**, **Battleship** and **Agar.io**, with lobbies open to your group, guild, realm or a private code.
- Solo games with you / guild / realm leaderboards: **Tetris** (official rules), **Snake**, **2048**, **Minesweeper**, **Flappy Bird**, **Wordle**, **Space Shooter**, **Candy Crush** and **Angry Birds**.

### Everything else
- One window in WoW's own style, a tab per running game, games keep going while you switch tabs.
- Practice with bots in every multiplayer game.
- Rules for every game; Hand rankings for poker.
- Statistics, Settings (mute, window size, minimap button, clear data), `/fng` to open.
